NocFree & RMK

Open firmware for the NocFree AND split keyboard.

Companion guides you through installing RMK on the left half, right half, and dongle. It saves the original firmware before installation.

Download Companion or view the source on GitHub.

The app

What it does

  • Type over USB, Bluetooth, or the dongle.
  • Change keys with Vial.
  • Choose ANSI, ISO, JIS, or KR in Companion.
  • Restore the original firmware from your saved backup.

Download Companion

The app includes the firmware. Choose your computer:

  • macOS (Apple silicon)
  • Windows (64-bit)
  • Linux (64-bit)

On Mac, you can also use Homebrew:

brew install --cask sarimabbas/tap/nocfree-rmk-companion
